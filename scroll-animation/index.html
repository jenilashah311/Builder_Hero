Our 4-Stage Home & Commercial Building Process.

From initial blueprint study to turnkey key handover, every milestone is supervised by Er. Ajay H. Shah with transparent rate calculations and structural rigor.

01

Soil Survey & Foundation

Thorough site inspection across Manjalpur, Akota, or Makarpura. Soil bearing testing, 2.1M depth footing excavation, and anti-termite treatment.

STAGE 1: GROUND ZERO
02

Heavy RCC Framing
